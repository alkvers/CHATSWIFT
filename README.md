## ✨ What is ChatSwift?

**ChatSwift** is a private and highly customizable messaging app designed around two simple ideas:

1. **Your conversations should stay yours.**
2. **The app you use every day should feel like your own.**

Instead of relying on a phone number, ChatSwift is built around a **username-based identity**. It combines modern messaging, voice and video calls, stories, groups, privacy controls, and deep visual customization in one lightweight Android experience.

No ads. No tracking. No unnecessary noise.

# 🛡️ Privacy by Design

ChatSwift puts control closer to the user.

Control what other people can see.

- Hide online status

# ❓ FAQ

### Do I need a phone number?
ChatSwift is positioned as a **username-based** messenger, so the public experience is designed around using a username rather than making a phone number your visible identity.

### Are messages permanent?
The public product information describes a **24-hour auto-delete** experience by default for chats and temporary shared content.

### Can I change how the app looks?
Yes. ChatSwift includes **5 complete app designs** and **6 color themes**, along with personalization options such as chat wallpaper and text sizing.

### Is ChatSwift free?
Yes. The app is presented as **free**, with **no ads** and no hidden charges mentioned in the provided product information.

### Can I control my privacy?
Yes. Available controls include hiding online status, managing read receipts, blocking users, controlling friend requests, and deleting your account.
