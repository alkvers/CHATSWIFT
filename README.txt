📱 APP KYA HAI?
ChatSwift ek messaging app hai jo privacy aur apni
marzi dono deti hai. WhatsApp jaisi chat app — lekin
phone number nahi, username se chalati hai, aur har
chat 24 ghante baad khud delete ho jati hai.
