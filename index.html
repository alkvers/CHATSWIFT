What is Seffni?

A messenger that puts control closer to you.

Instead of relying on a phone number, Seffni is built around a username-based identity. It combines modern messaging, voice and video calls, stories, groups, privacy controls and deep visual customization in one lightweight Android experience.

No ads. No tracking. No unnecessary noise.

FAQ

Looking for help?

Do I need a phone number?

No. Seffni is a username-based messenger, so the experience is designed around using a username rather than making a phone number your visible identity.

Are messages permanent?

No. Chats and temporary shared content use a 24-hour auto-delete experience by default.

Can I change how the app looks?

Yes — 5 complete app designs and 6 color themes, plus chat wallpaper and text sizing.

Is Seffni free?

Yes. The app is free, with no ads and no hidden charges.

Can I control my privacy?

Yes. You can hide your online status, manage read receipts, block users, control friend requests and delete your account.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 7.0 (Nougat) and above, with Wi-Fi or mobile data.
